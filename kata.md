
Objective of this website: provide our students a step by step guide to learn and exercise the kata.   

It uses the data provided in the obsidian PKM in a structured format per kata.    There can be multiple modes on the website:   summary overview of the kata,    step by step guide of the kata,    interactive mode where a student is tested on specific knowledge.  (this last feature is for later)

The step by step guide should give the user the ability to step through the kata with a mobile device and get an explanation step by step.    It would be great if the cue can also be given via text to voice from the browser.    At this point,  the interaction can be based on selecting a kata and clicking through it.    In future,  a timing based function could be made. 

The 'direction' item in a step is a description of the direction. 
The 'AngleFrom' is the orientation at the start of the step,  considering the start position of the kata is facing direction°
The 'AngleChange' is the change in orientation,    when it is positive, the direction is clockwise,  when negative,  the direction is anti clockwise
The 'step' indicates a relative 'short' or 'long' that could be used for visual cues.   short = shoulder length,     long = double shoulder length,   very long = double should length + extra footlength.  

The GUI should follow the same look and feel as used on the programma.html overview and the quiz.
